Common questions

Is the trial really free?

Yes — a full 20-minute lesson, no card details and no obligation to continue.

Do we need an instrument first?

Not for the trial. For lessons we recommend a 61-key touch-sensitive keyboard as a minimum; we will advise on models.

How are fees paid?

Monthly, by UPI, bank transfer or card. Invoices and receipts are available in the portal.
