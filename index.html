Welcome to SriKeys

A music school built around the student, not the syllabus.

SriKeys began with a simple belief: a child who enjoys practising will always outpace one who is made to. We teach technique and theory with rigour — and we protect the curiosity that made the student want to play in the first place.

Every student follows a personal learning plan, receives weekly practice notes, and performs at least once a year. Parents can follow attendance, homework and progress from the student portal at any time.

Meet the founder

“Technique is the vehicle. Expression is the destination.”

Sridevi Raman has taught piano for over fourteen years, preparing students for ABRSM and Trinity examinations and for the far harder test of playing for the people they love.

A performer and accompanist by training, she founded SriKeys to give every learner the kind of unhurried, individual attention she was lucky enough to receive. Her students range from four-year-olds meeting the keyboard for the first time to adults returning after decades away.

Our philosophy

Play with purpose. Practise with patience.

Music education is not only about correct notes. It is about listening, persistence, and the confidence to perform something imperfectly and begin again. We build those habits deliberately:

  • Small, honest steps. A clear goal for each week beats an ambitious plan for the year.
  • Technique serves expression. Scales matter because of the music they unlock.
  • Performance is part of learning. Every student plays for an audience, however small.
  • Parents are partners. Transparent notes, attendance and progress, always.
