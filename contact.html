Before you write

Quick answers.

Where exactly is the studio?

On Sardar Patel Road in Adyar, a two-minute walk from the IIT Madras main gate and close to Adyar bus depot. There is a small nameboard at the gate; the studio is on the first floor.

Do you teach online outside India?

Yes. We currently teach students in Singapore, Dubai, the UK and the US. Lessons are scheduled in IST with times that work across time zones.

Can I visit before enrolling?

You are welcome to visit during teaching hours. Write ahead so we can keep a quiet room free for a conversation.

How do I get my invoices and receipts?

Every enrolled family gets a student portal login. Invoices, receipts and payment history are all there, along with attendance and report cards.
