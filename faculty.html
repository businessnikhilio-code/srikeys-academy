Our teachers

Musicians who love to teach.

A small faculty, deliberately. Every teacher at SriKeys is a performing musician with a teaching qualification and the patience to explain the same idea three different ways.

Founder & Principal Teacher

Sridevi Raman

LTCL Piano · ABRSM Grade 8 (Distinction) · B.Mus

Sridevi has taught piano for fourteen years and accompanied choirs and soloists across Chennai. She specialises in building a secure classical technique in young beginners, and in coaxing adult returners back to the instrument without judgement. Her students hold a 96% ABRSM and Trinity pass rate.

Music Theory & Aural

Karthik Venkatesh

ABRSM Grade 8 Theory · M.A. Musicology

Karthik makes theory feel like a set of shortcuts rather than a set of rules. He teaches notation, harmony and form to students from Grade 1 to Grade 8, and runs the academy's listening and musicianship workshops. He also composes for short films.

Keyboard & Contemporary

Ananya Nair

RSL Grade 8 Keys · Performer & Arranger

Ananya plays keys for a Chennai-based indie band and teaches contemporary keyboard, chord voicings and improvisation. Her students learn to play the songs they actually listen to — and to understand why those songs work.

Teaching standards

What every SriKeys teacher signs up to.

  • A written plan per student. Reviewed every term against the student's goals.
  • Notes after every lesson. What was covered, what to practise, what to listen to.
  • No surprise exams. Families are consulted before any entry form is filled.
  • Professional development. Annual training in pedagogy and child safeguarding.
  • Honest feedback. Kind, specific and never inflated.
