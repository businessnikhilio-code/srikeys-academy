Questions

Before you enrol.

Do I need a piano at home?

A digital keyboard with 61+ weighted or semi-weighted keys is enough to begin. We will advise on the right instrument for your budget and space during the trial class — no purchase is required before lessons start.

Can adults learn from scratch?

Absolutely. Roughly a third of our students are adults, many starting with no notation at all. Lessons are paced for the time you actually have.

How much practice is expected?

Fifteen to twenty minutes, five days a week, is more effective than one long session before the lesson. We set practice that fits the student's week.

What happens if we miss a class?

Let us know at least 12 hours ahead and the lesson is rescheduled within the same month at no cost. Cancellations inside 12 hours are counted as a missed class, with one goodwill make-up per term.

Are online lessons as effective?

For students aged seven and above, yes. A second camera angle on the hands plus shared notation covers what a teacher needs to see. For ages four to six we recommend in-studio lessons where possible.
